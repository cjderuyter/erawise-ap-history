Contemporary Era

2010s – Present
Polarization, technology, and global competition redefine American life in real time.
Big Idea
The contemporary U.S. is shaped by extreme political polarization, the dominance of Big Tech and social media, the rise of China, climate change, and renewed debates over race, gender, immigration, and democracy itself.
