Age of Exploration & Early Contact

1492 – 1607
European contact triggers the Columbian Exchange — transforming both worlds through goods, people, and disease.
Big Idea
The collision of European, African, and Indigenous worlds created a new global economy — but also unleashed catastrophic disease, conquest, and the transatlantic slave trade.

Key Terms

  • Columbian Exchange — transfer of plants, animals, diseases
  • Encomienda — Spanish forced-labor land grants
  • Conquistador — Spanish military conqueror
  • Mercantilism — economic theory: colonies enrich the mother country
  • Black Legend — narrative of Spanish cruelty
  • Mestizo / Casta system — racial hierarchy in Spanish colonies

Key People

  • Christopher Columbus — 1492 voyage
  • Hernán Cortés — conquered Aztec Empire
  • Francisco Pizarro — conquered Inca Empire
  • Bartolomé de las Casas — defended Indigenous rights
  • Jacques Cartier — French explorer of St. Lawrence
  • Sir Walter Raleigh — sponsored Roanoke

Key Events

  • Columbus reaches Caribbean 1492
  • Treaty of Tordesillas 1494
  • Smallpox & disease devastate Indigenous populations (up to 90% in some regions)
  • Spanish conquer Aztecs 1521 & Incas 1533
  • St. Augustine founded (oldest U.S. city) 1565
  • "Lost Colony" of Roanoke 1587
  • Atlantic Slave Trade begins
Legacy & Connection
Sets the stage for English colonization, racial slavery, and centuries of conflict over land — patterns that shape the entire colonial project.
