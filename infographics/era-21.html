Vietnam & Social Upheaval

1960s – 1975
An unwinnable war abroad collides with a youth-led counterculture and rights revolution at home.
Big Idea
A long, divisive war in Vietnam, combined with sweeping social movements (counterculture, women's lib, environmental, Chicano, gay rights), shattered Cold War consensus and reshaped American politics, culture, and trust in government.

Key Terms

  • Domino theory — communism would spread
  • Gulf of Tonkin Resolution 1964
  • Tet Offensive 1968 — turning point
  • Vietnamization (Nixon)
  • Pentagon Papers 1971
  • Counterculture / Hippies
  • Great Society — LBJ's reform program
  • War on Poverty, Medicare, Medicaid 1965
  • Stonewall Riots 1969 — gay rights
  • Roe v. Wade 1973
  • 26th Amendment — voting age 18 1971
  • EPA created 1970

Legacy & Connection
"Vietnam Syndrome" makes Americans wary of foreign wars; the cultural divisions of the 60s still shape today's political polarization.
